How it works

Set up next to your B1.

TFP Vantage sits next to your SAP Business One and reads from it. Nothing in B1 is changed and nothing needs to be re-typed.

  1. Connect, read-onlyA read-only SQL login that can open only your company's B1 database. Your data is copied each night for fast answers; some answers, like item stock, are read live.
  2. Ask in plain wordsType a question the way you would ask a colleague, in English or Bahasa Malaysia, on a PC or a phone.
  3. Get the answerFigures, tables and charts with a short explanation and a next step, and Excel files to share.
Security

Your data stays yours.

Built for finance and sales data from the start.

Read-only

It uses a SQL login that can only read. It can't post, change or delete anything in SAP Business One.

On your own server

The AI model runs on your server. Your B1 data isn't sent to outside AI services.

Sign-in and roles

Each person signs in. Roles decide what they see: sales, purchasing, finance or warehouse.

Companies kept apart

Several B1 companies can share one sign-in page, but each has its own login, data and chats.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Which SAP Business One does it work with?

SAP Business One on Microsoft SQL Server. Ask us about your version.

Can it change anything in SAP?

No. It reads only, through a SQL login that has no permission to write.

How up to date are the answers?

Your B1 data is refreshed every night, and the app shows how old the figures are. Some answers, such as an item's stock, are read live.

Does it understand Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes. Ask in English or Bahasa Malaysia; it answers in the language you used.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The live demo uses SAP's public sample company, OEC Computers, so you can ask real questions before connecting your own data.

How much does it cost?

From RM 90 per user a month on your own server (minimum 5 users), or from RM 2,500 a month + RM 90 per user hosted by us, plus a one-time setup from RM 3,000. Every company is different, so we tailor the price to your users, companies and support needs: ask us for a quote.
